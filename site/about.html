About this site

What this site is

TravelPromoHub is a public index of travel offers from major travel brands. It gathers publicly listed offers in one place so they can be compared, and links every offer back to the brand’s own official page.

How the offers are checked

Each brand’s official offers page is checked on a fixed schedule. The wording and conditions shown for an offer come from the brand’s own official page. Every page shows a review date — that date is when that page was last checked.

What this site is not

This site is not the official site of any travel brand. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any of the brands listed here. All brand names, offers and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Who runs it

This site is an independent index, published under the name TravelPromoHub.
