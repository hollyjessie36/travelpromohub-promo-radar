Privacy Policy

What this site is

TravelPromoHub is a public index of travel offers. Each offer links to the brand’s own official page, where the offer, its conditions and its price are set and controlled by that brand.

Advertising and affiliate links

This site may display third-party advertising and may contain affiliate links. Where advertising or affiliate links are shown, they are supplied by third parties, not by this site. Those third parties may set their own cookies or identifiers and handle information under their own privacy policies. This site does not control what those third parties do, and their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

Offers, prices and terms

Offer content, prices and terms are set by each provider, not by this site. This site only indexes publicly listed offers and records when each offer was checked against the provider’s official page. This site does not sell travel services and takes no part in any transaction. Any booking or purchase is made directly with the provider, under that provider’s own terms.

Changes

This policy is updated when what the site does changes. The current version is always the one published on this page.
